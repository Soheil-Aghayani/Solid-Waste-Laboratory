BUILT FOR TRACEABILITY

Evidence you can inspect.

Results use a small, curated organic-chemistry reference set. Sample-specific material references and local reference comparison are included. Quantitative identification and a bundled mineral spectrum library are not included.

How results are ranked

Direct range overlaps come first, followed by nearby bands within your tolerance. Equal-distance results use narrower reference ranges first. This ordering is not a confidence score. Band shape, intensity, sample environment, and supporting bands are not inferred.

Peak detection & limitations

Candidate peaks are local maxima in absorbance or minima in percent transmittance. Prominence is measured against the higher of the two surrounding contour bases, stopping at a higher peak or the spectrum boundary, and scaled to the full intensity span. This is a simple heuristic, not validated peak fitting. Endpoints are excluded; noise, broad bands, and sampling density can affect detection. Optional smoothing uses the selected sample window; the plot always preserves the raw trace. Zoom into a range, select a candidate to inspect it, or edit its position. Enable chart picking to add a measured position to the review.
